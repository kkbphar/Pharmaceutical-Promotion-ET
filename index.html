🏢 About K2 Pharmaceutical Sales and Promotion ET

K2 Pharmaceutical Sales and Promotion ET is a pharmaceutical sales, promotion and networking platform designed to connect pharmaceutical suppliers with pharmacies, drug stores and other qualified buyers in Ethiopia.


Our platform helps suppliers promote their pharmaceutical, healthcare and cosmetic products while making it easier for buyers to discover products and submit orders.


💊 Pharmaceutical Products

Promotion and presentation of pharmaceutical products from suppliers.

🏢 Suppliers

A platform for suppliers to showcase their products and reach potential buyers.

🏪 Buyers

Pharmacies and drug stores can discover products and submit orders.

💄 Cosmetics

Promotion and visibility for cosmetic and personal-care products.


Our Vision

To become a trusted digital platform connecting pharmaceutical suppliers and buyers and improving access to product information and business opportunities in Ethiopia.


Our Mission

To provide a simple, professional and accessible platform for pharmaceutical product promotion, supplier networking and buyer-supplier communication.
