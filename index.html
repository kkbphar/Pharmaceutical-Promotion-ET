🏢 About K2 Pharmaceutical Sales and Promotion ET

Building connections across Ethiopia's pharmaceutical market.

K2 Pharmaceutical Sales and Promotion ET is a pharmaceutical sales, promotion and networking platform designed to connect suppliers with pharmacies, drug stores and other qualified buyers across Ethiopia.


The platform supports pharmaceutical product promotion, supplier visibility, buyer connections, product discovery and order requests.

🌍 Our Vision

To become a trusted digital connection platform for pharmaceutical suppliers and buyers in Ethiopia.

🎯 Our Mission

To make pharmaceutical product promotion, supplier networking and buyer connections easier, faster and more professional.
