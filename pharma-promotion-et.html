How it works

The same platform, two sides of the same trade.

For suppliers

01
Create your company profile
Add your license and registration details so buyers know you're verified.
02
List your products
Generic and brand name, manufacturer, strength, dosage form, pack size and availability.
03
Receive buyer inquiries
Respond directly through messages or WhatsApp and Telegram.

For buyers

01
Search or post a request
Find a listed product, or post exactly what you need and how much.
02
Compare verified suppliers
Check location, categories offered and verification status.
03
Contact and finalize
Reach out directly to agree on price, quantity and delivery.

Get featured placement for your company or product

Premium supplier accounts and featured listings put your profile at the top of search and on the homepage.
